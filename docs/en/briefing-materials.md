# Vision 🚀

Bocao aims to become the modern operating system for restaurants in LatAm and beyond.

Most restaurant software feels outdated, slow, and fragmented. Bocao combines AI, automation, communication, and operations into one clean platform designed for modern restaurants.

The goal is not only management — but intelligent assistance.

---

# Core Concept

A multi-tenant AI-powered SaaS platform for restaurants that centralizes:

* Orders
* Reservations
* WhatsApp communication
* AI customer support
* Kitchen operations
* Analytics
* Staff workflows
* Automation
* Marketing
